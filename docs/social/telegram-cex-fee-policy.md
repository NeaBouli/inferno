IFR EXCHANGE FEE POLICY — TRANSPARENCY UPDATE

On 26 August 2026 at 00:17 MET, the five-member IFR Core Developer and Keyholder Council adopted this policy by a 4-1 vote: transfers to and from officially verified centralized-exchange operational addresses will be fully fee-exempt.

Once a specific exchange address is activated on-chain:
• no 2.0% sender burn
• no 0.5% recipient burn
• no 1.0% pool fee

Important current status: the policy is approved, but no CEX address is fee-exempt yet. Each exchange must first prove control of its exact Ethereum addresses. Activation then requires a public TreasurySafe 3-of-5 Governance proposal, the 48-hour timelock and execution on Ethereum Mainnet.

Internal exchange trades are off-chain ledger entries and do not invoke the IFR token contract. Only Ethereum deposits, withdrawals and wallet movements are affected by the on-chain exemption.

Every activated or revoked CEX address will be published transparently in the IFR documentation and remains independently verifiable on-chain.
