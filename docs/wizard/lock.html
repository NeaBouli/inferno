Lock IFR for Benefits

Lock IFR in IFRLock to reach a public benefit tier. Partners that accept IFR read your tier directly from the chain and decide which benefit they offer for it.

Locked tokens remain yours. They are not spent; you can unlock them at any time, and your tier ends when you do. A tier is checked again at every use.
Bronze
1,000 IFR
Entry tier for partner benefits
Silver
2,500 IFR
Second tier; partners may offer more for it
Gold
5,000 IFR
Third tier
Platinum
10,000 IFR
Highest tier
Thresholds are locked IFR (9 decimals) from the public tier data ifr-benefits-tiers.v1.json. Benefits, discounts and their limits are set by each partner, not by the protocol. Locked IFR also counts as weight in advisory Forum votes at an announced snapshot block; it gives no Council or Safe rights.
