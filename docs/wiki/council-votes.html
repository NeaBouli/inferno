Council Votes

Public record of every Council ballot: the exact ballot texts, each signed vote, and the current tally.

Procedure: open ballots only. Each eligible Safe signer signs the published ballot text with the signer wallet on Etherscan Verified Signatures. Every signature is checked with ecrecover (EIP-191) and recorded on this page. A ballot never executes anything by itself: any on-chain action still needs its own Safe transaction and, where applicable, the 48-hour Governance timelock.

Rules

  • Eligibility: the Safe signers of the Treasury, Community and LP Reserve Safes (identical 3-of-5 owner set), fixed when a ballot opens. A Council registry may replace this snapshot later.
  • Display: signers appear only with consented initials. Wallet addresses are public on-chain; no real names are published.
  • Deadline: none, unless a deadline is approved by a separate Council vote.
  • Abstentions are recorded but not counted as votes. Only their signature hash and Etherscan link are listed.
  • Status colours: Open before the threshold is reached, Approved once it is reached, Executed after the resulting transaction is executed and linked.
  • Secret ballots are in planning as a separate milestone: anonymous credentials or zero-knowledge membership proofs with a threshold tally. They need a threat model, scheme selection, an independent cryptography and privacy review, and a staging run before any real ballot. Until then all ballots are open.
  • Portal: a page that automates this procedure (connect wallet, sign the predefined text, automatic verification, publication) is planned.

How to Vote

  1. Open etherscan.io/verifiedSignatures and choose Sign Message.
  2. Connect your Safe signer wallet.
  3. Paste the ballot text of your choice from this page, unchanged.
  4. Sign (free, no gas), choose Publish, and send the link to the Council.
