# Sepolia test ballot FB-SEP-0001

Should the IFR Forum collector be confirmed as working end to end on the Sepolia
test network?

This is a test ballot on Sepolia. Voting weight is test IFR locked in a dedicated
Sepolia test IFRLock contract at the announced snapshot block. It has no effect on
mainnet, funds, contracts or governance permissions. Forum results are advisory;
the Council decides.

Choose yes, no or abstain. A wallet's last valid signed vote counts; issuedAt
orders its votes and the collector's receipt order breaks exact ties. Abstain
counts towards quorum and is excluded from the yes/no result.
